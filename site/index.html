Blocked In

Slide rectangular blocks along their lanes to clear a path for the red key block. Spinners turn when you tap them, magnets tow the block behind them, and in later worlds the exit gate patrols the wall. 200 levels across 5 worlds, with undo and hints. Drag with a finger or the mouse.
